# Ebu Gogo Decompression

## Source Material

The Ebu Gogo are creatures of Nage folklore from Flores, Indonesia. Key sources:
- Gregory Forth, 1998 (documented tales 6 years before *H. floresiensis* discovery)
- Richard Roberts, discoverer of *H. floresiensis*, recorded village elder accounts
- Wikipedia and folklore databases

## Physical Description (Consistent Across Sources)

- Small stature (~1m tall)
- Long arms, hairy bodies
- Flat noses
- Long, pendulous breasts (detail no paleontologist can verify from bones)
- Bipedal
- Cave-dwelling

## The Three Motive Layers

The stories contain **three distinct justifications** for child abduction:

### Layer A: "Learn to Cook"
Some legends say the Ebu Gogo kidnapped children *hoping to learn from them how to cook*. The children always outwitted them.

### Layer B: "Cook Them"
Other versions say they kidnapped children *hoping to learn how to cook them*.

### Layer C: "They Just Eat Everything"
Richard Roberts recorded that the Ebu Gogo *ate everything raw* — vegetables, fruits, meat, and "if they got the chance, even human meat." The extermination was triggered when they *stole and ate one of their babies*.

## The Ambiguity Is the Signal

"Learn to cook" vs "cook them" is **not** a translation error. It is **narrative compression of two truths**:

1. The Ebu Gogo lacked fire/culture (hence "learn to cook")
2. They were opportunistic predators who would eat human children raw (hence "cook them" — or rather, NOT cook them)

The children "outwitting" them encodes the **cognitive asymmetry**: *Homo sapiens* children, even young ones, possessed symbolic reasoning and deception that the Ebu Gogo lacked.

## The Extermination: Oral History of Genocide

The Nage say the extermination happened "around seven generations ago" (~150-200 years, or 18th-19th century). The method:

1. Villagers offered palm fiber for clothes
2. Ebu Gogo accepted (they wanted clothes but couldn't make them)
3. Villagers threw a firebrand into the cave
4. Ebu Gogo burned alive

This encodes two technologies simultaneously:
- **Clothing**: requires symbolic self-representation ("how I appear to others")
- **Fire**: the wedge technology that separated sapiens from all other hominins

The extermination was performed **with the boundary technology itself**.

## The Geographic Problem

The Ebu Gogo stories belong to the Nage, 100+ km from Liang Bua (*H. floresiensis* site). But:
- Oral traditions can migrate
- Multiple hominin populations may have existed across Flores
- The Lio people of eastern Flores told Forth in the 2010s that *lai ho'a* — similar creatures — were "still occasionally seen," with accounts as recent as 2019

## Connection to *H. floresiensis*

*H. floresiensis* survived until ~50,000 years ago (revised dating). The Nage stories are more recent. They are not remembering *H. floresiensis* directly. They are remembering **a more recent hominin population** — or the cultural memory of **competition with any non-sapiens hominin** compressed into a single narrative.
