# Global Little People Gradient

## The Universal Template

Every culture describes the same creatures. The rendering varies by cultural complexity:

| Culture | Entity | Relationship | Cognitive Status |
|---------|--------|-------------|------------------|
| Nage (Flores) | Ebu Gogo | Predatory, non-negotiable | Subsapiens — insufficient recursive depth |
| Irish/Scottish | Aos Si / Fairies | Competitive, negotiable | Parallelsapiens — equal depth, different values |
| Scandinavian | Trolls | Status-seeking, substitutive | Parallelsapiens |
| Japanese | Tengu (kotengu) | Wild, punitive | Parallelsapiens |
| Japanese | Tengu (daitengu) | Pedagogical, wise | Supersapiens |
| Sumerian | Anunnaki | Creator/servant relationship | Supersapiens |
| DMT Space | Machine Elves | Pedagogical, information-bearing | Supersapiens |

## The Gradient Explained

The template is consistent: small, humanoid, intelligent, mountain-dwelling, capable of teaching or preying upon humans, associated with thresholds and forbidden knowledge.

The **Ebu Gogo** are the **bottom of the gradient** — the most primitive, direct, predatory. They don't substitute. They don't teach. They take.

The **DMT entities** and **daitengu** are the **top** — pedagogical, voluntary, information-bearing.

## The Failed Harvest

In Ra's framework, the Ebu Gogo are 2nd-density beings who did not make the 3rd-density transition. In Wrangham's framework, they are hominins who never made the **cooking transition**.

They are the **control group** in the cultivation experiment. The stories of child-kidnapping are about **the bypassed lineage's attempt to acquire the threshold technology retroactively** — by stealing the children who already possess it.

## Why Children Specifically

Children are the **test case for theory of mind and deception**. Even young sapiens children (3-5 years) develop false belief understanding. The Ebu Gogo could physically overpower children but could not **out-think** them. They lacked recursive cognitive architecture.

The kidnapping is **diagnostic**: not labor (no use for workers), not reproduction (no sexual intent), but **cognitive specimen collection**.
