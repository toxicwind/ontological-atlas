# DMT Entity Research

## Johns Hopkins Survey (n=2,561)

Key findings from the Davis et al. survey:
- 69% of respondents received a message from entities
- 19% received predictions about the future
- Most common descriptors: "being," "guide," "spirit," "alien," "helper"
- More than half of pre-experience atheists no longer identified as atheist afterward
- Rated "among the most meaningful, spiritual, and psychologically insightful lifetime experiences"

## Cross-Cultural Consistency

The entities are not hallucinations. Hallucinations are idiosyncratic. These are:
- **Consistent** across cultures with zero cross-contamination
- **Information-bearing** (they teach, predict, guide)
- **Ontologically transformative** (change fundamental beliefs)

Researchers explicitly noted similarities to "religious, alien abduction, and near-death contexts."

## The Rendering Filter

The entities appear as:
- Machine elves (Western psychonauts)
- Ancestors (indigenous shamans)
- Aliens (UAP experiencers)
- Angels (religious mystics)

Same signal. Different cultural equalizer settings.

## Connection to Default Mode Network

DMT disrupts the default mode network (DMN) — the brain's self-referential processing system. This is the same network implicated in:
- Ego dissolution (psychedelics)
- Mystical experiences
- Near-death experiences
- Schizophrenia (hyperactive DMN)

The DMN is the **neural correlate of the subject-object split**. Disrupting it dissolves the boundary.
