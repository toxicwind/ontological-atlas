# Somatic Bypass (Music)

## The "Rules" Being Broken

Western music theory is a **representational framework**. It describes melody, harmony, voice leading — all processes in the symbolic cognition layer.

Dubstep and trap "break the rules" by **descending below the symbolic layer** and operating directly on the body.

## Neuroscience of Bass

Bass sounds **selectively enhance cortical activity at beat-related frequencies** through a mechanism that bypasses early cochlear processing and recruits:
- Motor planning regions
- Cerebellum
- Basal ganglia
- Vestibular networks

This is not "listening to music." This is **neural entrainment to a physical field**.

## Uninvestigated Frequency Ranges

### A. LFO Wobble as Theta Entrainment
Dubstep's signature "wobble" modulates low-pass filters with LFO in the **2-10 Hz range** — the exact band as **shamanic drumming** (4-5 Hz, theta) and **delta brainwaves** (2-3 Hz).

No peer-reviewed study has examined whether dubstep LFO rates are functionally equivalent to shamanic drumming in entraining thalamocortical pathways.

### B. The Infrasound Boundary (<20 Hz)
Modern sound systems can reproduce infrasound. Research shows infrasound:
- Elevates cortisol
- Increases negative appraisal
- Stimulates vestibular system (sensations of "presence" or unease)

Some dubstep/trap productions push energy into this range. The effect is **ontological** — it makes the listener feel "something is happening" without locating it as sound.

### C. Vestibular-Auditory Coupling at >95 dB
Direct vestibular activation by bass occurs only above ~95 dB SPL. Club environments routinely exceed this. At these levels:

> The vestibular system — which governs balance, spatial orientation, and self-location — is being driven by an external source.

This is a **literal dissolution of the bodily boundary** between self and environment.

### D. The "Double Drop" as Phase Transition
The double drop modulates frequencies in the 8-100 Hz range, where "most brain emotions and sensory function operate." Described by producers as a "mind control effect."

## Music Theory as Guardrail

Music theory doesn't investigate these frequencies because it was developed to describe **works** — bounded objects created by composers (agents) and consumed by listeners (subjects).

It cannot accommodate music that:
- Is engineered as neurophysiological stimulus
- Requires physical coupling to large-scale sound systems
- Modulates autonomic state rather than resolving harmonically
- Is experienced as temporary dissolution of self-location

## The Somatic Bypass

Certain musics are not art objects. They are **ontological tools** — technologies for temporarily dissolving the consensus rendering of reality and replacing it with a coupled state where the listener's physiology is entrained to an external rhythm.

The shamanic drummers knew this (4 Hz = theta = journeying). The rave cultures rediscovered it accidentally.
