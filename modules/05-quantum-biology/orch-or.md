# Orch OR (Orchestrated Objective Reduction)

## The Theory

Penrose and Hameroff propose that consciousness arises from **quantum computations in microtubules** — protein structures inside neurons.

Key claims:
- Consciousness is not computational but **orchestrated quantum collapse**
- Microtubules function as quantum computers
- Anesthesia works by dampening microtubule quantum effects

## 2024 Experimental Support

A study in *eNeuro* confirmed:
- Microtubule-stabilizing drugs (taxanes) increase resistance to anesthesia
- Volatile anesthetics bind to microtubules and dampen quantum optical effects
- "Overall the Orch OR theory has more explanatory power, biological connection, and experimental validation than classical theories"

## The "Warm and Wet" Objection

Critics argued quantum coherence is impossible in biological tissue (too warm, too wet).

**Rebuttal**: Tegmark's 2000 attack used wrong separation distance (nanometers instead of femtometers). Corrected calculations show coherence times sufficient for Orch OR (10^-6 seconds).

## The Pseudoscience Letter (2023)

124 researchers signed a letter declaring IIT (Integrated Information Theory, which implies panpsychism) "pseudoscience."

Signatories included Dennett, Churchland, Baars — old guard of computational/materialist consciousness studies.

**The irony**: Templeton-funded adversarial collaboration (2023-2025) found:
- GNWT predictions failed
- Two of IIT's three predictions passed
- *Nature* editorial condemned "pseudoscience" language

The letter was a **territorial marker** sprayed by a paradigm under threat.
